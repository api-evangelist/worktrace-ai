# Worktrace AI

> Worktrace AI is an enterprise workflow-automation platform that discovers, reimagines, and automates repetitive work. A desktop application observes work across tools to find high-ROI automation opportunities, surfaces process-optimization recommendations aligned to company KPIs, and generates blueprints for building automations on third-party platforms such as n8n, Crew AI, and Asteroid. Backed by 8VC; serves financial services, healthcare, high tech, insurance, logistics, and telecom.
